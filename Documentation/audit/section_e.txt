### P2 — Long-term (Full migration to phase0)

**P2-1: Migrate CombatAI to phase0 combat rotations**
CombatAI (742 lines) is the largest engine file. Its `makeDecision()` → `CombatDecision` flow should be replaced by the phase0 `CombatRotation.selectSkill()` interface + `RotationFactory` + class-specific rotations. This is a major refactor that touches FleetPlay, Phase0Driver, and all combat tests.

**P2-2: Migrate QuestAI to phase0 QuestExecutor**
QuestAI (252 lines) should be replaced by `QuestExecutor`'s state machine. Requires updating AIBrain's `handleQuest()` to call QuestExecutor instead.

**P2-3: Migrate MerchantAI to phase0 town package**
MerchantAI (202 lines) → `BuyManager` + `SellManager` + `TownBehaviorEngine`. Requires updating AIBrain's `handleMerchant()`.

**P2-4: Migrate SocialAI to phase0 social package**
SocialAI (242 lines) → `SocialBehaviorEngine` + `ChatResponder` + `PartyInviteHandler`. Requires updating AIBrain's `handleSocial()`.

**P2-5: Migrate AIBrain to Phase0Brain**
AIBrain (262 lines) is the legacy decision loop. It should be replaced by `Phase0Brain` + `StateMachine` + `BotPlayController`. This is the capstone refactor — once done, the engine/ directory can be dramatically shrunk to just the infrastructure classes (GameServerClient, AIPlayerConnection, AIPlayerEngine entry point).

**P2-6: Evaluate advanced/neural/economy/social packages**
These packages (12 files, ~1,300 lines) are wired into AIPlayer but are aspirational in nature:
- `advanced/`: EmotionalState, PersonalityProfile, AdaptiveLearner, ReinforcementEngine — all are in-memory simulations with no persistence
- `neural/`: DeepLearningCore, PatternMemory — "deep learning" is actually a simple weighted-average predictor, not real ML
- `economy/`: EconomicEngine, MarketEngine, NetWorthOptimizer — all are singletons with simulated market data
- `social/`: DiplomacyEngine, CollectiveKnowledge, SwarmCoordinator — all are singletons with simulated diplomacy

Recommendation: Keep for now (they ARE referenced from AIPlayer and tested by Stream tests), but document them as "simulated intelligence" rather than real AI/ML. Consider replacing with phase0 equivalents as the phase0 system matures.
