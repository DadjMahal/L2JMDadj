## Problems & solutions
1. **"No GGAuth reply" right after an LS restart** — transient race; the probe hit the LS before it
   finished loading. Fix: restart the LS then wait for `:2106` to listen + 8s settle; or just run the
   probe after the LS has been up a while (login works when settled).
2. **Probe hung after CharSelected** — NIO `SocketChannel` blocking read ignores `Socket.setSoTimeout`
   (no read timeout), so it blocked forever when the server paused. Fix: classic `java.net.Socket`
   + `DataInputStream.readFully` (honors `setSoTimeout`, throws `SocketTimeoutException`).
3. **0 NPC_INFO after CharSelected** — the client MUST send `EnterWorld`(0x03) after CharSelect to
   spawn; without it the GS stays in ENTERING state. Added `sendEnterWorld` (105-byte payload).
4. **Player dead / no monsters at spawn** — the 25 `ai_%` chars were created at (16600,17000,434),
   NOT on a real map (void → dies, curHp=0). Moved CombatBot_01 to the Talking Island Wolf zone and
   healed it (`UPDATE characters SET x=..,y=..,z=..,curHp=maxHp,curMp=maxMp`).
5. **False "COMBAT PROVEN"** from counting `STATUS_UPDATE`(0x0E) (idle players get it). Fixed to require
   `ATTACK`(0x05) or `DIE`(0x06).
